Examples

Example gallery

Worked calculations with complete input files, from exact checks to comparisons with published simulations.

Each example lists what it tests, the numbers it produced, and its complete input file. To run one, save the input to an empty folder, change into that folder and call iDFT("input.yaml") (see Install). Reported residuals were checked independently after each run.

Lengths are in bead diameters d (= l_sc) unless an example sets physical units.

Example 01

Ideal gas in a hard-wall slit

Exact & consistency checks1Dideal

The simplest possible inhomogeneous calculation: an ideal gas between two hard walls. With only the ideal free-energy term, the equilibrium density is the Boltzmann profile, so SoftDFT can be compared with the exact answer at every grid point.

  • Maximum absolute profile error about 7×10−18 (machine precision).
input.yaml
procedure: iDFT
geometry:
  coordinate_system: Cartesian
  dimensions: [10.0]
  bin_width: [0.1]
  external_field:
    hard_walls:
      dims: [1]
species:
  - sequence: "A"
    density: 0.05
monomers:
  A:
    diameter: 1.0
    valence: 0.0
system:
  temperature: 298.15
  dielectric_constant: 78.4
numerical:
  max_iters: 100
  tole: 1e-9
model:
  - ideal

Example 02

Three-dimensional ideal gas in a patterned external field

Exact & consistency checks3Dexternal field

A user-supplied external potential that varies along x, y and z (including a mixed x+y+z term) is read from file on a 9×9×9 grid. The ideal-gas density must equal ρb exp[−βV(x,y,z)] in every cell, which tests the 3D grid indexing and the input_external field reader.

  • Bulk density ρbd3 = 0.1; the local density ranges from 0.039 to 0.258 across the box and varies along all three axes.
  • Potential mapping error: 0.
  • Maximum density error against the exact formula: 8.3×10−17.
input.yaml
procedure: iDFT
geometry:
  coordinate_system: Cartesian
  dimensions: [4.0, 4.0, 4.0]
  bin_width: [0.5, 0.5, 0.5]
  external_field:
    input_external:
      dims: [1, 2, 3]
      file: potential.csv
species:
  - sequence: "A"
    density: 0.1
monomers:
  A:
    diameter: 1.0
    valence: 0.0
system:
  temperature: 298.15
  dielectric_constant: 78.4
numerical:
  max_iters: 100
  damping: 0.5
  mixing_max: 0.5
  tole: 1e-11
model:
  - ideal

Example 03

Binary hard-sphere mixture: bulk pressure

Exact & consistency checksbulkmFMT

A bulk (iLST) calculation for a two-component hard-sphere mixture. The compressibility factor from SoftDFT's excluded-volume model is compared with the analytical Boyle–Mansoori–Carnahan–Starling–Leland (BMCSL) equation of state.

  • State point: ρAd3 = 0.08 (dA = 1.0), ρBd3 = 0.06 (dB = 1.5), packing fraction 0.148.
  • SoftDFT Z = 1.8281429558181252; BMCSL Z = 1.8281429558181255.

Example 04

Bulk thermodynamic consistency

Exact & consistency checksbulksquare well

A neutral A/B mixture with different diameters and ideal, mFMT and square-well terms. Each density is perturbed by ±10−6; the central-difference derivative of the excess free energy is compared with SoftDFT's excess chemical potential, and the pressure identity P = Σρi − fex + Σρiμex,i is checked.

  • Maximum derivative discrepancy 7.2×10−12.
  • Bulk pressure βPd3 = 0.103623 from SoftDFT and from the identity; discrepancy 0.
input.yaml
procedure: iLST
species:
  - sequence: "A"
    density: 0.03
  - sequence: "B"
    density: 0.06
monomers:
  A:
    diameter: 1.0
    valence: 0.0
  B:
    diameter: 1.2
    valence: 0.0
pairs:
  square_well:
    A, A:
      energy: 0.5
      lambda: 1.5
    A, B:
      energy: 0.2
      lambda: 1.5
    B, B:
      energy: 0.3
      lambda: 1.5

  temperature: 298.15

Example 07

Hard-sphere wall: density sweep and comparison with MD

Hard spheres1DmFMTMD comparison

Bulk densities 0.305, 0.701 and 0.857 are each solved on four grids (0.2d to 0.025d). The contact error shrinks roughly quadratically with grid spacing. Profiles at the exact state points of Davidchack, Laird & Roth are then compared with their published molecular-dynamics density profiles.

  • Profile RMSE vs. MD over 0.5–5d: 0.05% (ρ=0.305), 1.8% (0.701) and 8.6% (0.857) of bulk density.

Example 17

Charge-regulated walls

Charge regulation1DFFT PoissonpH

Each wall carries ionizable sites (Γ = 0.02 e/d2): acid sites on the left and base sites on the right, both with pKa 7. The site charge follows log[α/(1−α)] = ln10·(pH − pKa) + βeψwall (sign reversed for bases) and is solved self-consistently with the ion densities and the FFT Poisson solution. The reservoir pH is taken from the proton species Y.

  • Wall charges move from ±0.0100 (zero-potential guess) to ±0.00879 e/d2.
  • Charge-law residual 3×10−10; full DFT residual 1.2×10−7.
input.yaml
procedure: iDFT
geometry:
  coordinate_system: Cartesian
  dimensions: [12.0]
  bin_width: [0.2]
  external_field:
    hard_walls:
      dims: [1]
    charge_regulated_walls:
      dims: [1]
      site_density: [0.02, 0.02] # maximum magnitude, e / l_sc^2
      kind: [acid, base]         # left negative, right positive
      pKa: [7.0, 7.0]           # base uses conjugate-acid pKa
species:
  - sequence: "c"
    density: 0.02
  - sequence: "a"

Charge regulation1Dmixed walls
