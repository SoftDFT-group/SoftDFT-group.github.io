Contributing

Contributions are welcome through pull requests: new functionals, external fields, examples and documentation fixes. Please describe the physics, add an example input with a check against a known result, and report the final residual.

SoftDFT

Open-source classical density functional theory for complex and confined fluids, developed in the Gallegos group, Department of Chemical & Materials Engineering, New Mexico State University, Las Cruces, NM.
