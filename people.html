People

People

SoftDFT is developed in the Gallegos group, Department of Chemical & Materials Engineering, New Mexico State University, in collaboration with the Wu group at the University of California, Riverside.

Group

Dr. Alejandro Gallegos

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor, Chemical & Materials Engineering

Dr. Gallegos leads the development of SoftDFT and the Ising density functional theory on which it is built. The group develops molecular theories for chemically responsive soft matter: weak polyelectrolytes, polymer brushes, and the adsorption of amino acids and peptides at surfaces. Ph.D. in Chemical and Environmental Engineering, University of California, Riverside (2022).

NMSU faculty listing Google Scholar agal@nmsu.edu

Dr. Jianzhong Wu

Co-Principal Investigator · Distinguished Professor, Chemical & Environmental Engineering, University of California, Riverside

Dr. Wu serves as a mentor and theoretical lead for SoftDFT, guiding the choice of soft-matter systems so that new capabilities are both theoretically sound and useful to the community. His research develops statistical-mechanical methods, in particular density functional theory, to predict the microscopic structure and properties of confined fluids, soft materials and biological systems. Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering, University of California, Berkeley (1998).

Group website UCR profile

Dr. Reza Ghanavati

Postdoctoral Researcher

Joined the group as a postdoctoral researcher; his role is to co-lead SoftDFT development, from the code's architecture to new capabilities such as charge-regulated surfaces and electrostatics, coordinate the project's software engineering, documentation, tutorials and workshops, and mentor student researchers. Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering, New Mexico State University (2025). His doctoral research built polarizable, flexible force fields for molecular dynamics and Monte Carlo simulations of metal–organic frameworks for gas separation. His background covers quantum chemistry, materials modeling, machine learning, Python and high-performance computing.

GitHub Google Scholar rgh@nmsu.edu

SoftDFT
